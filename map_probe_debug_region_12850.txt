CURRENT PROBE RENDER PATH
-------------------------
1. Terrain scene-tile geometry/colors/UVs are cached once at viewer startup.
2. Terrain world vertices are CPU-projected each frame.
3. Loc definition/model transforms + contouring are cached as world-space vertices once.
4. Per-instance bounding spheres reject off-screen locs before vertex projection.
5. FOV/focal-length math is computed once per frame, not once per vertex.
6. Large triangle/geometry/projection scratch buffers are reused across frames.
7. Visible terrain + loc faces still expand into one ProjectedTriangle vector.
8. A global painter-sort still sorts all emitted triangles by average depth.
9. Consecutive same-texture runs still submit through SDL_RenderGeometry.
10. No occlusion culling or hardware depth buffer yet.

QUICK INTERPRETATION
--------------------
High terrain_ms now mostly means terrain projection/emission cost.
High loc_models_ms now means visible-instance projection/triangle emission cost.
Compare loc_instances_culled vs rendered to judge frustum-culling effectiveness.
High sort_ms: the global painter sort is the next architectural bottleneck.
High submit_ms or many geometry_batches: texture-fragmented SDL submission is expensive.
High present_ms: backend/vsync/display presentation is blocking.
